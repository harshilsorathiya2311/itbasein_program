FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about YourDrive.

How do I book a test drive?
Simply browse our car collection, select a car you're interested in, and click "Book Test Drive". Choose your preferred date, time, and submit. You'll receive a confirmation once the dealer approves your booking.
Is the AI recommendation accurate?
Our AI recommendation engine uses multiple machine learning models trained on extensive car data. It considers budget, brand preference, fuel type, transmission, and more to suggest the best matches. You can also compare recommendations across different algorithms.
Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?
Yes, you can cancel pending bookings from your "My Bookings" page. For rescheduling, please cancel the existing booking and create a new one with your preferred date and time.
Is YourDrive free to use?
Absolutely! Browsing cars, comparing specs, getting AI recommendations, and booking test drives are completely free. You only pay the dealer when you decide to purchase a vehicle.
How do I compare multiple cars?
Navigate to our car listing page and check the "Compare" box on at least 2 cars you're interested in. A comparison bar will appear at the bottom — click "Compare Now" to see a side-by-side view of features, specs, and pricing.
